# Fluid changes

## 0.1.0-alpha · November 2026

The first Fluid: fluency reading practice, as a web page and an Android app, built from Odin 0.10.0's fluency practice.

- **Practice** (the whole passage, with help: a misread or stuck word is said, the reader says it back, then praise) and a
  **one-minute reading** (like DIBELS 8: a word said after a 3-second stop), scored as words correct per minute and
  accuracy, with each misread word, word said for the reader and word left out marked on the passage.
- Odin's 36 passages, kindergarten to grade 5; **new passages** written with AI (on a school computer, a free tier or
  paid; the key kept only while Fluid is open) or typed in, kept on the device.
- Speech recognized **on the device only**: Chrome's on-device recognizer on a computer, Android's in the app (Odin's
  speech bridge). **Watch a sample reading** works without a microphone.
- **Nothing about readers is kept:** no sign-in, no names; the session's readings are in memory only.
- Tests: the page (a sample reading; practice and a one-minute reading with a stand-in recognizer; new passages with a
  stand-in AI; nothing kept) and an emulator check of the app (the bridge, Android's speech and voice, a sample reading,
  the AI through the app).
